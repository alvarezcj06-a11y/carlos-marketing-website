Blog

5 simple website tips that make a page easier to use

A website does not have to be complicated. A few simple choices can make it much easier for people to understand what a business or person is offering.

1. Make the message clear at the top

The first thing people see should tell them what the page is about. A clear title and short sentence can do a lot.

2. Give people one clear next step

Too many buttons can make a page confusing. One main button helps visitors know what to do next.

3. Make important things easy to notice

Headings, spacing, short sections, and simple design can help people scan the page without getting lost.

4. Make sure it works on a phone

A lot of people visit websites from their phones. Text, links, and buttons should still be easy to read and use on a smaller screen.

5. Use real information to build trust

Real experience, class projects, useful information, and clear contact options can make a website feel more trustworthy.
